## State model (controlled/uncontrolled, defaults, transitions)

- Open state lives on `Drawer.Root`; the swipe area requests transitions via `onOpenChange(true, details)` and never calls `onOpenChange(false, …)` when a controlled parent rejects the open `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:566-572`
- Default state is closed: the part renders `data-closed` `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:314`; after a swipe-driven open-then-dismiss cycle it returns to `data-closed` `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:972-977`
- State attributes on the part: `data-closed` (closed), `data-open` (open), `data-swiping` (only while a gesture is actively tracked; removed after release) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:930-946`, `data-disabled` (when `disabled`) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:685`
- Mid-gesture opening: once the gesture locks to the open direction and displaces, the drawer opens *during* the drag (before any pointerup) — popup shows `data-open` at 60px displacement with a 100px-tall popup `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:714-737`, at 40px with a 0-height popup `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1228-1239`, and at 80px with a 200px-tall popup `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1440-1459`. The popup is mounted and `data-swiping` by 40px displacement with a 100px-tall popup `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:647-671`. A completed 10px locked swipe still fired `onOpenChange(true)` `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:566-571` (the minimum lock displacement is UNVERIFIED — inferred from `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:566-571` + `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1668-1684`, no test pins it between 0 and 10px)
- Release decision (commit vs revert) is distance-based for slow gestures, against a size-based default threshold: with a 200px-tall drawer, a slow 70px swipe reverts to `data-closed` while a slow 120px swipe stays open — threshold is therefore >70px and ≤120px (35%–60% of height; exact value unasserted) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1338-1371` (Chromium-only). Velocity participates in the release decision only within an 80ms window of the last move (`MAX_RELEASE_VELOCITY_AGE_MS`, per test comments) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:108-111`, `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1353-1357`; a velocity-only open is UNVERIFIED — inferred from those comments, no test directly asserts a velocity-only commit
- Direction locking: a gesture whose dominant displacement is off-axis (60px horizontal vs 2px vertical on a bottom drawer) never locks, never opens, and never calls `onOpenChange` `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:321-336`; a zero-displacement (stationary) gesture also never opens or calls `onOpenChange`, on move or up `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1657-1694`
- Gesture lifecycle transitions: press → track (moves) → `data-swiping` → release (commit open, or revert closed); `pointercancel` aborts and resets (`data-closed`, no `data-swiping`) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:813-829`; a mid-gesture `buttons` change (primary released) ends the gesture leaving the drawer open `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1241-1249`; a `contextmenu` also ends the gesture leaving it dismissible by outside press `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1311-1335`
- Mid-gesture `disabled` flip: the swipe-progress reset (`swipeProgress → 0`) is applied synchronously in the layout phase, before passive effects flush (regression-guarded via a `visualStateStore.set` probe) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:673-683`; the popup loses `data-swiping` `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:684`; an already-open drawer stays open through the disabled flip and a later pointerless release click `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:737-752`
- After a swipe-open, outside-press dismissal is initially suppressed for clicks not preceded by a fresh `pointerdown` (the release-click guard), and re-enables on a fresh pointer press (mouse `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:948-977`, touch `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:979-1010`, and after interrupted gestures `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1255-1261`)

## Keyboard interactions

- No keyboard interaction on the swipe area itself is tested (no focus/tabindex/keydown behavior asserted for the part).
- After a swipe-open, a keyboard `Enter` activation on an outside button dismisses the (non-modal, `initialFocus={false}`) drawer — i.e. the release-click guard does not block click-only activations that never had a pointerdown `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1128-1157` (Chromium-only)

## Focus management

- In a non-modal drawer (`modal={false}`) with `initialFocus={false}`, opening by swipe does not move focus: the previously focused outside button retains focus across the swipe-open `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1145-1150`
- No other focus behavior (focus trap, return focus) is asserted by this file for the swipe area.

## Accessibility (roles, aria-*, id linking)

- No `role` or `aria-*` attributes are asserted for the swipe area anywhere in this file; it behaves as a plain `div`.
- The part carries a generated `id` from `useId`; when `useId` returns `undefined` the `id` attribute is absent and swiping still works `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:832-849`
- The swipe area registers itself in the dialog store's element registry (`store.context.triggerElements`) keyed by id once a generated id becomes available (React 17-style effect-resolved id included): `getById(id)` returns the element `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:851-884`
- An id change updates the registry: the old id entry is removed, the new id maps to the element, and the registry holds exactly one entry `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:886-908`

## DOM structure & portal behavior

- The part itself renders in place as a `div` (no portal) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:298-303`
- `Drawer.Portal` content (backdrop, viewport, popup) mounts on open and unmounts on close; `keepMounted` keeps the popup mounted while closed `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:546-562`, `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:689-691`
- During a swipe the following inline CSS custom properties are written:
  - Popup: `--drawer-swipe-movement-x` / `--drawer-swipe-movement-y`; the perpendicular axis is actively zeroed (`'0px'`, not removed) for a horizontal drawer `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:421-424`; the popup's `style.transition` is `'none'` while swiping `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:425`
  - Backdrop: `data-swiping` during the gesture (removed after release) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:426-427`, `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:455-458`; `--drawer-height` set to the popup's measured `offsetHeight` during the gesture `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:427`; `--drawer-swipe-progress` reset to `'0'` after release `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:456-458`
  - Provider `Indent`: `--drawer-swipe-progress` (`'1'` at/over full travel, `'0'` at none) and `--drawer-height` (popup height during gesture, cleared to `''` after) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:428-441`, `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:459-460`
- Overshoot (dragging past the fully-open point) is sqrt-damped ("rubber-band"): for a 100px-wide popup swiped 250px in the open direction, `--drawer-swipe-movement-x` is `-Math.sqrt(150)` (≈ −12.25) where 150 is the overshoot in px `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:416-424`
- Movement is computed from the popup's *rendered* transform distance, not just its size: with the popup at `translateX(60px)` (width 100) and a 40px swipe, `--drawer-swipe-movement-x` is ≈ −20 before release `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:463-504`; for a bottom drawer with height 200 grabbed at 80px displacement, `--drawer-swipe-movement-y` is `'120px'` (200 − 80 remaining travel) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1440-1461`
- When the popup has no measured height (`offsetHeight` 0) during an opening swipe, `--drawer-height` is omitted (empty string, not `'0px'`) on both backdrop and indent `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:506-539`
- When a controlled parent rejects the open, the mounted (closed, `keepMounted`) popup gets no `data-swiping` and its `--drawer-swipe-movement-y` stays `'0px'` — no swipe visuals leak into a rejected state `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:571-577`
- Mid-close re-grab (popup still mounted during a real exit animation, `BASE_UI_ANIMATIONS_DISABLED = false`): a single direction-locking move re-opens the drawer and writes the movement value on the same commit that flips `open` true; the viewport's open-reset must not clobber it back to `'0px'` (movement-y stays `'120px'`) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1373-1461` (Chromium-only)

## Events (names, payload shape, bubbling, preventDefault semantics)

- `pointerdown` (mouse/pen path): default-prevented for cancelable events (fireEvent returns false ⇒ `defaultPrevented === true`) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:338-355`; a non-cancelable `pointerdown` is never `preventDefault`-ed and left un-prevented `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:761-793`
- Pointer events with `pointerType: 'touch'` carrying real displacement are ignored for swipe-open ("compatibility" emulated-touch pointers): a `pointerdown/pointermove/pointerup/pointercancel` sequence of 80px displacement does not call `onOpenChange` `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:795-811`. Touch input is handled via touch events instead: `touchstart`/`touchmove`/`touchend` swipes open the drawer `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:910-928`
- `pointermove`: tracked while pressed; a move whose `buttons` no longer include the primary button ends the gesture (drawer stays open) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1241-1249`; a `buttons: 0` move with real displacement *finishes the gesture by itself* (no trailing `pointerup` needed) — the quick-flick case where the pointer lifts before the first move is sampled `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1468-1515`
- `pointerup`: commits the release; after a released-move flick already finished the gesture, a trailing real `pointerup` is a no-op — no double `onOpenChange`, no spurious close, `data-swiping` stays removed `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1517-1575`
- `pointercancel`: aborts the gesture and resets state (`data-closed`, no `data-swiping`) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:813-829`
- `contextmenu`: interrupts an in-flight swipe-open gesture; the drawer remains open and outside-press dismissal works afterwards `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1264-1336`
- `onOpenChange` payload: called as `(true, <event/details object>)` — the second argument is asserted only as `expect.anything()` (exact shape unasserted) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:571`; exactly-once semantics hold for a flick whose trailing `pointerup` also arrives `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1557-1574`; never called with `false` when the controlled parent rejected the open `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:572`
- Release-click guard (click bubbling to document/body): the `click` synthesized by the gesture's own `pointerup` (dragging past the popup releases the pointer over the backdrop) must not dismiss the just-opened drawer, even when it lands a macrotask later (after any timer-based re-enable would have fired) — proven for mouse, pen, and touch click pointer types `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1012-1055`; likewise after a mid-gesture disable where the pointer is still down at release `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:744-752`
- Guard interaction with real (trusted) input (Chromium-only): a trusted click arriving without a preceding `pointerdown` (pointerdown stopped at window capture, ahead of document capture) is suppressed once; the guard is then consumed, so the next deliberate outside click dismisses `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1057-1126`
- A pointerless *virtual* click (no pointerType; `isVirtualClick` classification) is allowed to dismiss when the swipe release produced no click (touch input path) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1159-1191`; keyboard activation likewise dismisses (see Keyboard) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1152-1156`
- No `stopPropagation` behavior is asserted anywhere in this file.

## Edge cases (rapid interactions, unmount, nesting)

- Quick flick, pointer path: a single `pointermove` with `buttons: 0` and no `pointerup` still commits the swipe-open, clears `data-swiping`, and the drawer is then dismissible by outside press `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1468-1515`
- Quick flick, touch path: the entire travel in one `touchmove` between `touchstart`/`touchend` still opens (first-move latency calibration must not discard single-move travel for the swipe area, which tracks no dragged element) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1577-1608`
- In-place press-release (a `buttons: 0` move with zero displacement, then `pointerup`) never opens and never calls `onOpenChange` `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1610-1655`; a stationary move on a horizontal drawer likewise never opens, on move or up `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1657-1694`
- Interrupted gestures leave the drawer open and dismissible: gesture ended by a `buttons`-change move (no up/cancel) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1193-1262` or by `contextmenu` `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1264-1336`; in both, a subsequent outside press dismisses and the part returns to `data-closed` `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:1255-1261`
- Mid-gesture `disabled` flip cancels tracking and swipe visuals (layout-phase reset, `data-disabled`) while a drawer already opened by the gesture stays open `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:679-691`, `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:737-752`
- Controlled rejection: no swipe state is applied or closed when `open={false}` is controlled and the parent ignores the request `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:541-577`
- Missing id (useId → undefined): part renders without `id` and still opens `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:832-849`; deferred id registration and id changes are handled (see Accessibility) `packages/react/src/drawer/swipe-area/DrawerSwipeArea.test.tsx:851-908`
