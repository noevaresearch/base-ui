## Focus management

- After a toast's root unmounts and remounts (list hidden then shown), the toast is re-registered as active/focusable: `{F6}` + `{Tab}` moves focus to the remounted root element, which is a different DOM node than the original `packages/react/src/toast/root/ToastRoot.test.tsx:383-395` (node identity asserted at `packages/react/src/toast/root/ToastRoot.test.tsx:385`).
